```text
PAGE 14 — NHALOS AHEAD

FULL-PAGE SINGLE-PANEL SPLASH

REFERENCE ROLES:
- Final Page 13 — primary continuity and rendering anchor: exact rider positions, secured resources, boot propulsion, twilight and dry comic finish.
- Nhalos technological motorcycle concept — exact vehicle identity only.
- Astronauta sheet — exact identity only.
- Farsight sheet — exact identity only.
- Bandit sheet — exact identity, sealed mask and boot propulsion only.

RENDER MODE:
Base Render — Classic Cel Shading.

NARRATIVE FUNCTION:
1. Provide a strong visual transition from wild Nhalos to inhabited Nhalos.
2. Show the trio traveling as a coordinated unit.
3. Establish the first distant view of a real Nhalos city.
4. Make the city feel modest and frontier-based rather than a huge advanced metropolis.
5. End the travel transition with commerce, workshops and society visibly ahead.

EMOTIONAL OWNER:
The trio, with the city as the page’s destination.

DOMINANT VISUAL DEVICE:
Extreme long-shot perspective and environmental scale.

PAGE ARCHITECTURE:
Exactly one full-page vertical splash panel.

No subdivisions, insets, decorative frames, montage fragments or additional panels.

COMPOSITION:
Use a distant rear three-quarter perspective from high above and behind the travelers.

In the lower-to-middle foreground:
- Astronauta occupies the forward riding position on the canonical technological motorcycle;
- Farsight rides directly behind him, brown jacket and purple visor clearly identifiable;
- the motorcycle travels at high speed along an elevated or winding Nhalos route;
- Bandit flies parallel to the motorcycle using controlled propulsion from both boot soles;
- Bandit remains completely masked, with respirator and both blue lenses sealed in place;
- the recovered Morphos components are securely carried without obstructing movement.

Astronauta may handle the motorcycle’s primary riding position while its autonomous systems maintain route guidance, stability and speed.

Bandit’s boot propulsion must use two restrained blue exhaust shapes directly below his boots. No wings, jetpack, flight aura, giant flames or costume redesign.

In the far distance:
Reveal the first complete view of a modest inhabited Nhalos city.

CITY IDENTITY:
The city is unmistakably urban but not highly developed, monumental or utopian.

It should include:
- low and medium-height industrial buildings;
- workshops and repair yards;
- cargo platforms;
- technological motorcycle traffic;
- market canopies and commercial corridors;
- communications antennas;
- modest hangars;
- stacked housing integrated into black rock formations;
- route lights and practical bridges;
- scattered cyan and violet technology accents;
- visible daily activity at a distance.

The city grew around trade, maintenance and frontier routes. It should feel inhabited, improvised, weathered and economically active.

Do not show:
- giant skyscrapers;
- immaculate futuristic towers;
- royal palaces;
- an enormous space capital;
- a dense cyberpunk megacity;
- military occupation;
- giant holographic advertising;
- recognizable Earth architecture.

The surrounding jagged Nhalos geology remains dominant enough to show that the city exists on a harsh planet.

Use the route as a strong leading line connecting the traveling trio with the city.

Mando is not visible in this panel. He is waiting within the city.

TIME AND LIGHT:
Same alien twilight as previous pages, but the city introduces warmer practical commercial lights against the restrained cyan/violet Nhalos palette.

The contrast should communicate a transition from isolation to social life without making the city look luxurious.

TEXT AND LOCATION POLICY:
Do not generate a location card yet because the city has no confirmed canonical name.

Once its name is defined, a location card may be added manually or the page prompt may be revised under the Elseframe location-card exception.

CONTINUITY:
- Exactly one panel.
- Astronauta rides in front.
- Farsight travels behind Astronauta.
- Bandit flies alongside using boot propulsion.
- Bandit remains fully masked.
- No breathable environment is established.
- Motorcycle preserves its canonical concept design.
- Mando waits in the city and does not appear.
- The city is modest but clearly inhabited and commercial.
- No named new character appears.
- No Captain Pirate, Matapobres, Freezer, Aurelia or future companion.

HOUSE STYLE:
2D hand-drawn American comic-book artwork, organic variable-width black ink contours, clear readable silhouettes, hard graphic cel shadows, controlled medium environmental detail, selective hatching, strong 2D depth through perspective and overlap, and dry matte comic-print color.

Use atmospheric separation only to distinguish foreground travelers, middle-distance route and distant city. Preserve hard ink structure. Maximum one broad deliberate highlight per material plane.

No photorealism, CGI, 3D render, glossy overrendering, painterly airbrush, excessive bloom, scattered highlights, wet-look surfaces, giant energy trails, lens flares or uncontrolled particles.

ABSOLUTELY NO GENERATED TEXT:
No title, dialogue, balloons, captions, narration, location card, sound effects, letters, numbers, readable signs, advertisements, logos, page number, watermark or signature.
```
